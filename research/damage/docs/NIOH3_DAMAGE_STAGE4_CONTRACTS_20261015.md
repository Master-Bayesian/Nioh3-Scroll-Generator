ordered aggregation, field materialization, post arithmetic, late source
mutation, attack/hit contexts and HP application. A single mathematical entry
can compose these stages, but a commutative attribute sum is insufficient.

  hard HP/agility/toughness constraint evaluation, and exact rescoring of every
  returned legal incumbent. Synthetic laboratory scores cannot certify this.

weights until real formulas justify a different objective. Report scenario

exact/discrete/stochastic/relaxed methods on the same real evaluator and budgets;
gradient usefulness and GPU speed remain empirical decisions. Existing synthetic
policy-gradient and exact-bound benchmarks stay useful engineering evidence but
