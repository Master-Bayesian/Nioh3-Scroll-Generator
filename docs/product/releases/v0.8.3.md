## Intended candidate

The proposed v0.8.3 slice adds save-file equipment generation from the game's seed-based item-grant route and extends equipment management. A generated item is a whole record produced from an item, rarity, seed, player difficulty/progress, and zero drop-source context; enemy and region drop variants are not covered. Per-slot legality alone is not the intended guarantee. No-match results are limited to this route. Yasakani Magatama's raw effect value remains `1` as requested by the owner.
