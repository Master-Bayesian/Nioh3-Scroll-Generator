## Intended candidate

The proposed v0.8.3 slice adds save-file equipment generation from the game's seed-based item-grant route and extends equipment management. A generated item is a whole record produced from an item, rarity, seed, player difficulty/progress, and zero drop-source context; enemy and region drop variants are not covered. Per-slot legality alone is not the intended guarantee. No-match results are limited to this route. Yasakani Magatama's raw effect value remains `1` as requested by the owner.

- The research record reports emulator-to-port comparisons of 3,600 compact
  entries and 5,000 full records, plus 1,500 comparisons across difficulty and
  progress thresholds. Raw receipts for the latter two counts were not retained
  in the handoff.
- The Rust parity fixture contains 900 records and compares whole records byte
  for byte. A retained run passed; this is offline parity evidence.
- The research record reports that 1,013 of 1,318 owner-save items matched a
  selected-field replay projection before the group-key repair. These counts
  have not been rerun against the repaired audit, do not establish every
  nonmatch's cause, and are not whole-record or in-game save/reload evidence.
- CE native insertion was demonstrated with the owner on September 26: a fan
  appeared in game and persisted to the inspected save; after correcting the
  script's code-cave overlap, a hell Bizen Uchigatana also appeared in game.
  This prior native-call evidence does not establish this tool's seeded
  save-add integration. See the linked live research record in CURRENT_HANDOFF.
- The retained backend checks also include the character-edit E2E, seeded
  equipment-search path and replay audit listed in the readiness record.
- A host integration case reproduced an audit false positive when only a
  stored effect group key changed. Replay now compares that key; the same
  integration case and more than 1,000 generated replay cases pass after repair.
- Seed-search results now expire when their inputs change, and late responses
  are discarded. The route-scoped mocked-bridge workflow passed 45/45 checks;
  TypeScript and the 1,115-message locale audit also passed.
- No live write or real-save test is scheduled for this intake. The owner set the
  maximum context boundary for a save-file workflow at returning to the title
  screen; shutting down the game is not required.
