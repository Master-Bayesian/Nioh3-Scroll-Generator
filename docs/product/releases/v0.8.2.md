capabilities currently spread across the owner's supplied cheat table, save
editor and equipment research. Inventory every supplied capability before
selecting implementation slices; do not silently omit difficult features or

ownership bounds, meaningful bounded E2E evidence and a repeatable artifact.
Persistent edits additionally need backup/readback and same-item save/reload
acceptance; temporary hooks need defined enable/disable/process-exit cleanup.
Keep unsupported operations unavailable rather than importing unchecked writes.
Preserve all existing scroll, backup, recovery and one-file update behavior.

No new product capability, native write, live-game acceptance or v0.8.1 package
is claimed by this intake record.

Owner product-scope update (2026-09-21). Browsing and editing owned
equipment/soul-core records are tracked as **one** feature: `EQUIPMENT-READ` is
its delivered read stage, and editing stays a future `EQUIPMENT-EDIT` slice, not
completed. Two new **planned** stable IDs are added in
[FEATURES.md](../FEATURES.md). `LEGAL-EQUIPMENT-GENERATION` covers two proposed
routes: native natural-drop generation under legitimate inputs/context, or
offline replay/inverse search of items attainable for requested effects; it is
explicitly not native inventory insertion of a custom 512-byte draft, and the
reviewed trainer-side add call shape does not establish a game-side ABI, RNG,
roll ranges or exclusions. `INVENTORY-LEGALITY-AUDIT` is an independent
read-only scan of scrolls and equipment that reports one of four honest outcomes
(proven reachable, known rule violation with a reason, no violation found within
documented checks, or insufficient data) and never brands provenance, promises
anti-cheat safety, or auto-repairs. Both share one versioned generation/rule
dataset. This is planned scope with evidence-dependent scheduling, not a
commitment that either ships in v0.8.1, and no implementation is added here.

External applications, bundled decrypted saves and third-party binaries are not
product dependencies. Source notices and data provenance are reviewed separately
before any redistribution. No application candidate or product version bump has
been made.
